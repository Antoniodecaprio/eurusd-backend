# Product Blueprint

The app should be a cohesive market-intelligence workbench, not merely a signal generator.

## Planned workspaces
1. **Market overview:** spot context, session, volatility, quality and freshness.
2. **Forecast:** probabilistic ranges/quantiles, horizons, fan charts, assumptions and timestamps.
3. **Technical analysis:** structure, trend, momentum, volatility, levels and indicators with limitations.
4. **Macro & news:** central banks, inflation, employment, growth, yields, calendar, geopolitics and risk sentiment; display event times in Kampala EAT (UTC+3).
5. **Scenarios & risk:** possible paths, invalidation, exposure, risk calculation and no-action state.
6. **Data transparency:** provider, timestamp, fallback status, missingness, candle conventions and source disagreements.
7. **Model health:** calibration, backtests, baseline comparisons, failure periods and version.
8. **Journal/learning (proposal):** plans, execution records, results, rule adherence and lessons.

## UX direction
Dark terminal-inspired UI was requested. Mobile readability, clear hierarchy and uncluttered charts are priorities. Separate facts, derived features, forecasts and user interpretations. Make horizon/time visible; never let stale forecasts look live. Show warnings and contradictions rather than hiding them.

Watchlists, alerts, replay, event briefings, personalized controls, journaling and multi-instrument comparison are candidate future features—not all verified as implemented.
