# Decision Evidence and Risk

## Evidence philosophy
Indicators may be correlated because they transform the same price series. Several agreeing indicators do not automatically constitute independent evidence. Weight evidence by quality, freshness, horizon relevance, independence and measured predictive value.

## Contradictions
Display disagreement. Explain what conflicts, input limitations and invalidation conditions. HOLD/no-action is appropriate when data is stale, model calibration weak, evidence insufficient, or conflict material.

## Signal outputs
BUY/SELL/HOLD are potential UI labels only. They require explicit tested rules and must state timeframe, conditions, uncertainty and invalidation—not promises.

## Risk principles
Demo-first. No martingale/averaging-down recovery default, confidence-based oversizing, or uncontrolled leverage. Any future sizing must account for risk per trade, aggregate exposure, daily/weekly limits, drawdown, correlations, account currency, pip value, spread, slippage, commission, financing, lot minimum/step, contract size and margin. Stops are not guaranteed fills. These safeguards are principles/proposals unless verified in code.

User context: learning trading, returned to demo after losses, requested discipline and risk planning. Do not encourage depositing or revenge trading. Live execution remains disabled.
