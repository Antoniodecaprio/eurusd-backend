# Constitution & Vision

## Mission
Build a serious, transparent, broker-independent market-intelligence and forecasting platform. EUR/USD is the initial proving instrument—not the product ceiling. The ambition includes data intelligence, probabilistic forecasts, technical context, macro/news, scenario analysis, model health, risk-aware decision support, and eventually broader assets/platforms.

## Principles
- Evidence beats confidence theatre; indicator count is not evidence strength.
- Forecasts are distributions/scenarios, not promises.
- Surface uncertainty, data freshness, source disagreement, assumptions and limitations.
- HOLD/no-action is valid when evidence is weak, conflicting, stale, or unvalidated.
- Preserve provenance and explain transformations.
- Keep research data separate from executable broker prices.
- Validation gates release; preserve failed results.
- Risk controls are foundational, not optional.
- Mobile-first: workflows should be usable on Android and small screens.
- Assistant ideas must be labeled as proposals until accepted.

## Scope
Begin with EUR/USD. Multi-asset expansion (FX, indices, commodities, crypto, etc.) is a future goal dependent on data access, licensing, and asset-specific validation. This is research/learning support—not guaranteed-profit software. Live execution is currently disabled.
