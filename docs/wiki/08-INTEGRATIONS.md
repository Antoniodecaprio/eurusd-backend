# Integrations and Ecosystem

Keep the research engine broker-independent; use adapters.

## Possible targets
- **MT5:** broker symbol/timeframe and future account/execution context; current integration not verified.
- **TradingView:** charting/alerts/data handoff possibilities; API/licensing research required.
- **cTrader:** possible future adapter; access/API/permissions research required.
- **Multiple brokers/providers:** broker metadata, symbol mappings and feed provenance.

Before any execution, model bid/ask, spreads, commission, slippage, financing, contract size, pip value, account currency, volume constraints, margin/leverage, order types, rejects and fills. External APIs, rate limits, redistribution, authentication and consent require research. FlutterFlow was explored for mobile; a prototype does not mean production completion.
